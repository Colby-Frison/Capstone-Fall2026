1.1.This TICKET is based on YOU Understanding the Domain: Understanding the project domain is a crucial step in software engineering. It ensures that the system you build aligns with the specific needs and requirements of the domain. To gain a deep understanding, follow the tasks below:
## a. Identify Key Questions (Group Work: 1 hours)
As a group, brainstorm and identify a minimum of five key questions that will help you understand the domain thoroughly. These questions should focus on the critical aspects of the domain, such as requirements, challenges, stakeholders, or goals.
## b. Research and Answer Questions (Individual Work: 6 hours)
Each team member independently researches the five questions and documents their answers. Be thorough and provide relevant, detailed insights.
## c. Collaborate and Refine (Group Work: 1 hours)
Regroup as a team and share your individual findings. 

Discuss your insights with your assigned Mentor/Client to clarify and refine your understanding of the project domain. As part of this discussion, determine the role your Mentor/Client will play (e.g., primarily as a client, primarily as a mentor, or a mix of both) and document their expected level of involvement. Prepare a clear plan for presenting both your findings and the defined Mentor/Client role to the class.
## d. Document and Submit (Group Work: 1 hours)
Document the artifact and submit it before coming to the presentation.
