# Problem Identification 
> [clearly define the problem or opportunity for which you are seeking a solution, and provide any relevant historical/background information]
## Problem: 
- Newer pilots who do not understand what an FAA medical certificate requires, how to obtain one, and the steps/hacks to get get through the process successfully.

## Sub problems:
- where do they obtain accurate information,
- what information needs to be disclosed on their medical,
- what happens if they disclose,
- how do they easily find the information that will help them,
- what happens if the physician they see (Aviation Medical Examiner) is unable to give them a medical certificate
- what do all those medical words mean?

---

# Project Objectives 
>[list/describe the desired outcome, problem resolution, end product or opportunity taken]

- Create a web platform/website/page (must be within FAA acceptable framework) that is easy for new pilots to find.
- Make it a page they want to use and easy to navigate.
- Fill it with information that makes sense to to a new pilot.
- With correct medical content (provided), develop tools to help new pilots obtain correct answers to their medical questions.

Make sure pilots know they should first get healthy, take care of their health, see their physicians if they do have a medical concern, understand medical concerns with being a pilot, then work on their medical certificate.

# Project Requirements 
> [define the business (why project is happening), solution (functional & non-functional), design specifications, and/or stakeholder requirements that align with the project's resources and objectives]

Increase in pilot medical certificate applications over past 2 years. They often have medical conditions including mental health issuesand think their pilot career is over before it starts.

## Solutions: 
Educate pilots on where to find accurate information on what they need to provide and what to expect in the process.

## Design specs: 
Continue the work that occurred in the fall 2025 and spring 2026 semesters with Dr. Abdulhak's student team. The previous student teams prototyped many different concepts including Al assisted searches, interactive graphics, and JSON files to demonstrate the art of possible with Al along with the accuracy of results and presented them to FAA CAMI leadership. The concepts are ready for more detailed work and potential integration with the FAA Office of Communication.

## Stakeholder requirements: 
Content should be accurate, easy to find, easy to understand and encouraging.
