# Ticket 1 — Key Domain Questions

Official research set for CS-4273 Ticket 1 (Domain Understanding). Each team member independently researches and answers **all five** questions.

## Domain research questions

1. **What is an FAA medical certificate?** Cover certificate classes (1st / 2nd / 3rd), who needs one, and why it matters for new pilots.

2. **What must applicants disclose, and what happens after disclosure?** Focus on MedXPress / Form 8500-8 requirements, especially mental health and common conditions that scare new applicants.

3. **How does a new pilot obtain a certificate end-to-end?** Steps from prep → application → Aviation Medical Examiner (AME) visit → issue / deferral / denial, and what to do if the AME cannot issue.

4. **Where should pilots get accurate information, and what does “FAA-acceptable” content mean for this project?** Official sources vs unofficial advice, and how that constrains the web platform.

5. **What did prior OU teams build (Fall 2025 / Spring 2026), and what should this semester continue?** Prototypes (AI search, interactive graphics, JSON content), accuracy findings, and handoff / integration direction with FAA Communications.

### Demoted (not part of the core five)
- Accessibility standards — note under Q4 if relevant; more implementation than domain.
- Regulation changes since prior development — fold into Q5 if found; otherwise confirm with the mentor.
